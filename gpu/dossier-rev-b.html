01 / EXTERIOR

Orthographic clearance

A case-fit check must account for the connector, exhaust path and expansion-slot access.

Envelope versus keep-out

The Quick Start Guide asks for space of 304 × 137 × 61 mm, including additional clearance relative to the nominal cooler body. Cable routing must also respect the connector manufacturer's bend and seating requirements; a side panel must not force a cable against the receptacle.

Interface checklist

Confirm the primary PCIe x16 slot is accessible, the chassis bracket matches the card, and adjacent cards do not obstruct the intake or outlet. Support the card during installation; a visual alignment animation does not certify bracket retention or sag.

No hole coordinates, tolerances, connector datum positions or measured thickness profile are available to MyStuff. Do not use this plate to machine a case or bracket.

02 / ASSEMBLY

Anatomy of the cooler

A layered interpretation of the three-dimensional assembly.

Why the packaging matters

A conventional graphics board can obstruct a portion of the cooler's projected area. By moving the main electronics toward the centre and separating some I/O functions, this design gives the two fan zones more direct access to the heat exchangers. That changes both the mechanical envelope and the airflow path.

Component functions

The fans generate pressure across fin passages; the frame and shroud constrain airflow and protect the assembly. Thermal interface materials reduce resistance between components and the spreader. The board supplies compute, memory and electrical conversion. These functions are coupled: a poor contact can raise die temperature even when airflow is adequate.

The diagram is an analytical illustration, not an instruction to disassemble a liquid-metal cooled card.

02 / ASSEMBLY

Three-board topology

The spatial split is central to the cooling architecture.

Electrical partition

The principal board houses the GPU package, surrounding memory and regulator circuitry. Separate board structures provide the host edge interface and display I/O, connected to the main assembly. This is an architectural decomposition, not a disclosed netlist.

Trade-offs

A compact board shortens some paths and frees cooler volume, but increases local component density, interconnect complexity, assembly tolerance demands and repair difficulty. High-speed links require controlled impedance and careful return-current paths through every interconnection.

Service boundary

The design cannot be faithfully reconstructed from an exterior model alone. Internal connector pinout, board stack-up, fastener torque and material specifications remain unavailable.

Configuration

The 5090 exposes 170 of 192 possible SMs, or about 88.5%. This distinction prevents theoretical full-die resources from being misreported as shipping hardware. Product configuration may reflect yield and segmentation decisions; the particular reason for any unit being inactive is not public.

04 / COMPUTE

Streaming multiprocessor

From instructions to arithmetic: the resource unit that schedules parallel work.

Parallel execution

GPU programs are divided into threads; hardware groups threads into warps. A warp executes a sequence of instructions while the scheduler switches among ready work to cover latency. Divergence, register pressure and shared-memory use can reduce effective throughput.

21,760 × 2 × 2.407 GHz ≈ 104.75 TFLOP/sIllustrative FP32 fused multiply-add peak at published boost clock. Two operations per FMA.

Real limits

Clock, instruction mix, occupancy and memory traffic determine realized performance. A peak arithmetic rate is neither a game FPS prediction nor a guarantee that each instruction stream runs at that rate.

04 / COMPUTE

Tensor and ray-tracing paths

Specialized units accelerate different classes of computation.

Matrix arithmetic

Tensor units target dense or structured-sparse matrix operations. Lower-precision formats reduce data size and may increase throughput, but supported kernels, accumulation precision and acceptable model accuracy determine the usable advantage.

Ray tracing

Ray-tracing hardware accelerates bounding-volume traversal and intersection tests. The application still performs material shading, texture access, sampling and denoising. The bottleneck can move from geometric tests to memory or shading.

Do not compare AI TOPS and FP32 TFLOP/s as if they were interchangeable. Precision, sparsity and accumulation assumptions differ.

04 / COMPUTE

Neural rendering and media

The frame pipeline is more than raw rasterization.

Reconstruction versus generation

Super-resolution estimates a higher-resolution image from lower-resolution rendering and auxiliary information. Frame generation synthesizes intermediate presentation frames. Both can improve perceived smoothness, but neither is equivalent to increasing native simulation rate or the response rate of input processing.

Fixed-function media

The 5090 has three ninth-generation encoding engines and two sixth-generation decoding engines. Dedicated video hardware can relieve general shader resources, subject to codec, resolution and application support. Media-engine counts do not translate directly into end-to-end editing speed.

This dossier uses launch-era architecture and documented feature families, not a promise about the latest driver or DLSS release.

GDDR7: bandwidth and capacity

Why the 512-bit interface changes the memory-bound operating envelope.

512 / 8 × 28 = 1,792 GB/sTheoretical decimal bandwidth at 28 Gb/s per pin; excludes transaction overhead.
Memory metricRTX 4090RTX 5090
Capacity24 GB32 GB
Bus width384-bit512-bit
Bandwidth1,008 GB/s1,792 GB/s

Capacity and working sets

Capacity allows a larger set of assets or model weights to remain local; bandwidth governs how quickly data can move. A memory-bound operation may benefit substantially, while a CPU-bound or compute-bound task may see much less. Framework and allocation overhead reduce the available capacity.

Bandwidth ratio: 1.778×; capacity ratio: 1.333×. These are specification comparisons, not benchmark results.

06 / ELECTRONICS

Board anatomy and signal integrity

A conceptual board map that distinguishes observed architecture from undisclosed routing.

Memory routing

High-rate channels place constraints on conductor impedance, crosstalk, timing skew, termination and power integrity. Designers coordinate stack-up, reference planes, vias and package connections so that signals remain within their electrical margins.

Power network

Switching converters feed several low-voltage domains. Local capacitors serve rapid transient demand while bulk capacitance buffers slower changes. High-density component placement affects copper temperature, mechanical stress and access for inspection.

Actual layer count, component designators, part numbers, layout files, power-rail voltages and timing constraints are not available; this is not a replacement PCB blueprint.

Power conversion and protection

The board-power figure describes the card as a system, not a single electrical rail.

575 W / 12 V ≈ 47.9 AIllustrative equivalent input current if the whole board drew from a single ideal 12 V source. Not connector current distribution.
P(loss) = I²RFor an illustrative 1 mΩ resistive path at 10 A, loss is 0.1 W. Resistance and current values are examples, not measured 5090 data.

Design considerations

Parallel regulator phases spread current and heat, and their control loop must respond to changing workload demand. The actual connector and slot share, regulator efficiency, transients and protection thresholds cannot be derived from the nominal TGP alone.

Do not infer safe wiring from these calculations. Use supported cables, connectors and PSU configuration specified by the manufacturer.

07 / THERMAL

Double flow-through

The mechanical and electrical layouts are co-designed to provide two cooling paths.

Heat-exchanger architecture

Both fan regions direct flow through the fin structure. The centrally located electronics reduce the area blocked by a long conventional PCB. Air entering the card is warmed as it absorbs heat from the fins; the system case must then remove that heat rather than circulate it back into the fans.

Air-side limits

Airflow depends on the fan pressure-flow curve and the resistance of the fin passages, filters, neighbouring cards and enclosure. Nominal free-air fan flow alone does not determine cooling capacity under load.

The plate shows an intended flow direction, not measured velocity vectors, acoustic predictions or temperature distribution.

07 / THERMAL

Heat transfer and thermal resistance

From junction to room air: a simple model of the coupled thermal path.

ΔT = Q × RθExample: 400 W through an assumed 0.10 K/W path gives a 40 K rise. Neither input is a measured die-path value.

Phase-change spreading

A vapor chamber redistributes heat by evaporation and condensation of a working fluid. A liquid-metal interface reduces contact resistance at the die-to-cooler boundary. The exact materials, chamber pressure, wick structure and interface thickness are not disclosed.

Energy balance

At an illustrative 575 W steady board load, the card contributes approximately 575 joules per second of heat to the environment, apart from small exported signals. The die, memory and regulators divide that load; using board TGP as die heat would overstate the junction path.

Thermal resistance examples are analytical teaching calculations, not a validated thermal model. No safe temperature, fan curve or thermal limit is inferred.

08 / PRODUCTION

Manufacturing and assembly

A plausible industrial process map, separated from the proprietary production route.

Semiconductor and board build

The die is fabricated and tested, then packaged for mounting to the board. A representative board assembly uses solder-paste deposition, pick-and-place, reflow and inspection. The board is then integrated with interconnects, thermal contact materials, cooling hardware and the enclosure.

Qualification and release

Electrical functional tests, power cycling, stress workloads, interface tests, mechanical checks and thermal/acoustic sampling would be expected in a robust release process. Test limits, process capability, actual sampling plans and supplier identities are not publicly established here.

Theoretical generational comparison

Architectural resource changes set an upper bound, not a universal application uplift.

QuantityRTX 4090RTX 5090
FP32 peak82.6 TFLOP/s104.8 TFLOP/s

Bandwidth1,008 GB/s1,792 GB/s

Interpreting ratios

The larger memory bus delivers a greater proportional bandwidth improvement than the nominal arithmetic peak. The increased TGP also matters: higher absolute performance does not guarantee improved energy per task. Actual efficiency needs paired performance and measured power from the same workload.

The 5090's boost clock and peak rates are conditional reference values. The chart does not claim that games or applications scale proportionally.

09 / PERFORMANCE

Measured workload differences

Independent launch-era tests demonstrate why one speedup figure cannot describe a GPU.

Workload dependence

Puget's launch review reported relatively modest gains in editing and real-time workloads and larger improvements in GPU-heavy offline rendering. Their reported results apply to the tested system, software builds and configurations; driver changes and scene complexity can alter the result.

Gaming

The Verge reported a 35% gain in Forza Motorsport with TAA in its own comparison. That is one game and setting, not a general 4K average. Frame-generation tests must be reported separately from native rendered-frame tests.

MyStuff's render chamber measures the browser running on the user's actual machine. It does not execute the benchmark on a virtual or remote RTX 5090, and cannot be used to validate these numbers.
