02 / Racing imagery & results

1995 marks the beginning of the Repsol Honda partnership. Honda's official archive identifies No. 1 Mick Doohan as champion with 248 points, Àlex Crivillé fourth with 166 and Shinichi Ito fifth with 127. Honda documents the 1992 Big Bang engine development and Doohan's first title in 1994.

03 / Virtual engineering exhibit

Two-stroke V4, four schematic expansion chambers, twin-spar frame, slicks, analogue cockpit, movable steering and brake lever, separable assemblies and foam-lined helmet case. The simulated key-start, horn, cash handover and private forecourt ride are creative adaptations. Engine sound is an original ElevenLabs-generated NSR500-inspired effect (not an archival recording), with synthesized fallback; RPM and transaction indications are not measured performance or genuine transactions.

Model geometry is procedural and approximate, not factory CAD, not a physical fabrication specification. No top speed or acceleration claim is simulated as measured data.
